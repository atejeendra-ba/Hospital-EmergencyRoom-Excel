# **Excel Dashboard Project: Hospital Emergency Room Analysis**
🚑 From raw data to real impact in healthcare…

I recently worked on a project where I analyzed emergency room data to answer a simple but critical question:

👉 How can hospitals reduce wait times and improve patient experience?

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of ER operations, helping stakeholders monitor performance, identify bottlenecks, and improve patient care.

🔍 **Key Highlights:**
• Total Patients: 513 with monthly trend analysis
• Average Wait Time: 36.32 minutes
• Patient Satisfaction Score: 4.96
• Admission Analysis: 52.44% admitted vs 47.56% not admitted 
• Patient Attend Status: 62% on-time vs 38% delayed
• Gender Distribution: 53% male, 47% female
• Age Group & Department Referral Insights for deeper analysis

📊 **Features:**
• Interactive monthly filtering (2023 vs 2024)
• Dynamic KPI tracking with trend sparklines
• Department-wise patient referrals
• Age-group segmentation for demand patterns

🛠 **Tools & Technologies:**
• Excel
• Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation) 
• Power Pivot (Data Modelling & DAX) 

📊 What stood out?
Delays, peak age groups, and referral patterns revealed clear opportunities to optimize operations.

💡 This project reinforced how powerful data can be in saving time, resources, and ultimately improving patient care.

💡**Outcome:**
This dashboard enables real-time monitoring, supports data-driven decision-making, and helps optimize emergency room efficiency and patient experience.
